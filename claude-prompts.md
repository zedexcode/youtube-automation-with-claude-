# Claude Prompts for YouTube Automation

Copy, paste, and adapt these prompts for your own niche. Always fact-check
the output before recording.

## Niche research

> "List 10 underserved sub-niches inside [NICHE] for a faceless YouTube
> channel. For each one, give 3 video ideas and one sentence on why it
> might attract viewers."

> "Analyse these 5 channels in the [NICHE] niche: [CHANNEL NAMES]. What
> topics do they repeat, what are they missing, and where is the gap a new
> channel could fill?"

## Video ideas

> "Give me 20 video title ideas about [TOPIC] for a faceless YouTube
> channel. Make them specific and curiosity-driven, not generic. No
> clickbait that the video could not honestly deliver."

## Script outline

> "Write a script outline for a YouTube video titled '[TITLE]'. Audience:
> general viewers with no background in the topic. Structure: hook, setup,
> 3 main sections, payoff. Keep each section to 2 bullet points. Do not
> invent quotes or statistics."

## Full script section

> "Write the hook (first 30 seconds, about 70 words) for a video titled
> '[TITLE]'. Spoken style, short sentences, no greeting, no 'in this video
> we will'. Start with the most surprising fact."

## Titles and thumbnails

> "Give me 10 alternative titles for a video about [TOPIC]. Keep each
> under 60 characters. Rank them by curiosity and clarity, and say why
> the top one works best."

> "Suggest 3 thumbnail concepts for a video titled '[TITLE]'. Each
> concept: one main visual, max 3 words of text, and the emotion it
> should create."

## Description and SEO

> "Write a YouTube description for a video titled '[TITLE]'. First 2 lines
> must contain the main keywords and the video's promise. Then add
> chapters, and finish with a one-line call to action. Keep it under
> 200 words."

## Fact-checking

> "Here is a script paragraph: [PASTE]. Flag any claims that look
> uncertain or commonly misreported, and tell me what I should verify
> before publishing."

## Repurposing

> "Turn this video script into a 60-second short script with the same
> hook and one key point: [PASTE SCRIPT]."
