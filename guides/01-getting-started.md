# 1. Getting Started: What YouTube Automation Really Means

## The basic idea

YouTube automation means building a channel where you do not appear on camera.
Instead of filming yourself, you produce videos using a repeatable workflow:

**Research → Script → Voiceover → Visuals → Edit → Upload → Improve**

AI tools like Claude help with the research and script stages. You still make
the creative decisions.

## What automation is NOT

- It is not "upload and get rich". Most channels take months of consistent
  uploads before they grow.
- It is not copying other people's videos. Reused or spammy content can get
  a channel removed from the Partner Program or banned.
- It is not fully hands-off. Even automated channels need a human checking
  quality, accuracy, and policy compliance.

## The tools you need (free to start)

| Stage      | Free options                          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Script     | Claude (free tier)                    |
| Voiceover  | Your own voice, or a free TTS tool    |
| Visuals    | Pexels, Pixabay (free stock)          |
| Editing    | CapCut (free), DaVinci Resolve       |
| Thumbnails | Canva (free)                          |
| Research   | YouTube search, Google Trends         |

## Your first milestone

Do not aim for monetisation on day one. Aim for this instead:

1. Pick one niche (see guide 02).
2. Publish 10 videos on a fixed schedule (for example, 2 per week).
3. Read your analytics after video 10 and double down on what worked.

Consistency beats perfection. Your first videos will not be great — that is
normal, and it is how every creator starts.
